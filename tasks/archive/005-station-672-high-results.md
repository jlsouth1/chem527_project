## Goal

Review the high post-2006 total-phosphorus results flagged at station 21AWIC-672, verify their source metadata, and decide whether they should remain in the primary analysis. Compare them with nearby station samples and document how much they affect the station summary.

## Progress log

- 2026-11-21: Inspected the raw records for 2015-10-07 and 2019-10-01. The results were 0.247 and 0.274 mg/L, respectively; both are labeled `Phosphorus`, fraction `Total`, have `Final` status, no qualifier, and use the same ADEM colorimetry method. Decided to retain both; an IQR flag alone is not evidence that a result is invalid.

## Outcome
Investigating other data points on the flagged days proved to be helpful, as it pointed to a generally high day for phosphorous counts across the Cahaba for all stations that were recording that day. The median for those dates on the stations (excluding the stations in question) were 0.106 mg /L for 2015-10-07 and 0.143 mg / L for 2019-10-01. These medians were 3.0x and 4.1x higher than the 35 ug / L limit, even without the samples in question. This points to two days of particularly high phosphorous count, and furthers the case to keep the samples in question for the overall calculus
